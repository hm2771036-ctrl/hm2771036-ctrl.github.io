1. CV Work Experience Rewrite

Role: Customer Operations / Administrative Officer

❌ BEFORE (Weak & Passive)

"Responsible for answering customer calls, handling complaints, and keeping records in the office every day."

Problem: Lists basic duties with zero measurable impact or keywords.
✅ AFTER (ATS-Optimized & Impactful)

"Resolved 45+ daily customer inquiries across phone and email with a 96% first-contact resolution rate, while streamlining digital record tracking to cut retrieval time by 30%."

Result: Shows scale, metrics, and keywords recruiters search for.

2. LinkedIn Headline Makeover

Role: Recent Graduate / Entry-Level Analyst

❌ BEFORE (Invisible to Recruiters)

"Graduate of Economics | Looking for a job | Hardworking and fast learner"

Problem: Recruiters never search the phrase "looking for a job."
✅ AFTER (Searchable & Value-Driven)

"Data & Business Analyst | Excel, SQL & Power BI | Turning Raw Data into Actionable Business Insights"

Result: Packed with exact job titles and tools recruiters filter by.

3. CV Professional Summary

Role: Healthcare / Care Assistant (UK & International Applications)

❌ BEFORE (Generic Clichés)

"I am a dedicated and compassionate team player seeking a challenging position in healthcare where I can utilize my skills and grow."

Problem: Could apply to anyone; doesn't highlight compliance or care skills.
✅ AFTER (Tailored & Credible)

"Compassionate Healthcare Assistant with 3+ years of experience delivering person-centered support, medication assistance, and safeguarding compliance in fast-paced residential care settings."

Result: Immediately establishes experience, core competencies, and readiness.
